UFC ANALYTICS / AN INTERACTIVE REPORT

From striking activity to final outcomes: explore what makes two fight cards different, and see the evidence behind every comparison.

Recorded data through May 16, 2026 · Missing stats excluded · Median excludes zero-takedown fights · Pre-2000 durations unverified · Charts use no minimum sample; the fighter finder starts at 5 fights (adjustable); counts shown · Snapshot, not a live feed.

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the UFC. This is a portfolio piece, not a prediction or betting tool: the charts describe past fights, and the Fight Game is a simulation, not a forecast.
