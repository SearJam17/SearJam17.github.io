Inside the Cage: sprite preview

A rough preview before the Phaser arena (milestone 2). Original template art, no real-fighter likenesses; names are data labels only.

Top: raw template. Bottom: recoloured in code (skin tone, corner fight shorts, referee in the middle). Both fighters share one floor line; the blue corner is mirrored to face the red corner. Scaled 3x with no smoothing.
